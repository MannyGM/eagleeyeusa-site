Data security in the AI era

AI changes how your data can be exposed

AI helps people work faster, and it helps attackers too. It can write convincing scam messages and imitate a familiar voice. Private information pasted into an AI tool can also end up outside your control.

  • Know what data you have and who can reach it
  • Set clear rules for what goes into AI tools
  • Use strong sign-in protection on every account
  • Keep networks and devices updated and monitored
